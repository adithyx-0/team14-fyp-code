Malayalam political meme triage

Upload a meme to see whether it trolls or supports, and who it targets. A decision aid for human reviewers, not an automatic verdict.

Drop a meme image here, or click to choose a file
How reliable is this? The model (frozen Swin-Tiny image encoder + MuRIL text encoder, small trained head) scored macro-F1 0.51 on intent and 0.39 on target on a 100-meme held-out test set — better than always guessing the majority class, but far from reliable. "Support" memes are rare in the training data (23 of 500), so they are often missed. The heatmaps show which image regions most influenced the prediction (Grad-CAM); in testing they were clearly more faithful than random regions, but they are coarse (7×7). Uploaded images are processed in memory and not stored.
